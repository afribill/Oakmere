About Oakmere

A Canadian wood care brand, made in Canada, for furniture and kitchen wood worth keeping.

For furniture worth keeping.

Good wood lasts generations, if it gets a little care. Oakmere exists to make that care simple: a few honest products, made well, that you can use in minutes.

Every tin and bottle is made in Canada in small batches, with Canadian beeswax, carnauba wax and food-grade mineral oil. No silicone and no added fragrance.

Oakmere is a brand of Denejo Inc., a Canadian company based in Kingston, Ontario.
