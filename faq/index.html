Frequently asked questions

Where to buy, what is inside, and how to get the best from Oakmere wood care.

Where can I buy Oakmere?

On Amazon. In Canada, every Oakmere product is on Amazon.ca. In the United States, the Beeswax Wood Polish & Conditioner and the Butcher Block Conditioner are on Amazon.com. The Cutting Board Oil is currently sold on Amazon.ca only. Every buy button on this site links straight to the listing.

Is Oakmere made in Canada?

Yes. Oakmere is a Canadian brand and every product is made in Canada, with Canadian beeswax. Oakmere is a brand of Denejo Inc., based in Kingston, Ontario.

What are the ingredients?

Wood Polish & Conditioner: Canadian beeswax, carnauba wax and food-grade mineral oil (USP).
Butcher Block Conditioner: Canadian beeswax and food-grade mineral oil (USP).
Cutting Board Oil: Food-grade mineral oil (USP) and beeswax.
Every Oakmere product is unscented and silicone-free.

What kind of oil do you use?

Food-grade mineral oil (USP) in every product. It is clear, unscented and does not go rancid, unlike olive oil or other cooking oils.

Do the products have a scent?

No fragrance is added. You may notice a faint, natural beeswax smell from the tins, which fades once the wax is buffed in.

Can I use the wood polish on finished furniture?

Yes. The Beeswax Wood Polish & Conditioner is made for finished and raw wood. On antiques, painted pieces or finishes you are unsure about, test a small hidden spot first.

Is the Butcher Block Conditioner safe for food surfaces?

It is a food-safe conditioner made with canadian beeswax and food-grade mineral oil (usp). Let it absorb, buff off the excess, and the board is ready to use.

What is the difference between the Cutting Board Oil and the Butcher Block Conditioner?

The oil is a pourable blend of food-grade mineral oil with a little beeswax. It soaks deep into dry wood, so it suits new, very dry or end-grain boards. The conditioner is a thicker wax balm that seals the surface and helps water bead. Most boards do best with oil first, then conditioner. See oil vs wax.

How often should I polish furniture?

Every few months for most pieces, and more often for high-touch surfaces like dining tables and railings. Polish again when the wood looks pale or dry. Dust in between.

How often should I condition a cutting board?

About once a month for a board in daily use, or whenever it looks lighter, feels dry, or water stops beading on it.

The balm looks creamy rather than golden. Is that normal?

Yes. Natural beeswax blended with oil sets to a soft, creamy colour in the tin. It goes on clear and buffs to a warm glow.

My tin softened in a hot car. Is it ruined?

No. Beeswax softens in heat and firms up again once it cools. Store the tin closed, away from direct heat and sun.

Can I use Oakmere on wood floors?

We do not recommend waxing floors, because wax can make them slippery. Use a product made for floors instead.
