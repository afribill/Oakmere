What it does

Oakmere Cutting Board Oil is food-grade mineral oil (USP) with beeswax, for wooden cutting boards, butcher block, salad bowls and kitchen utensils. It pours and spreads easily and soaks into dry wood, which makes it a good first step for new boards, end-grain boards and boards that have been neglected.

The oil conditions the wood and the beeswax helps protect the surface. It is unscented, so nothing transfers to food. For a thicker protective layer, follow it with Butcher Block Conditioner.

Where to use it

  • Cutting boards
  • Wooden utensils
  • Salad bowls
  • Butcher block

How to use it.

All you need is a soft, clean cloth.

  1. Clean and dry

    Wash the board and let it dry completely, standing on its edge.

  2. Spread the oil

    Pour a little oil on and spread it over every face, edge and end grain.

  3. Let it soak

    Leave it a few hours or overnight. Repeat on new boards until the wood stops absorbing.

  4. Wipe and buff

    Wipe off the excess and buff dry. For extra protection, finish with Butcher Block Conditioner.

Questions

What is the difference between the Cutting Board Oil and the Butcher Block Conditioner?

The oil is a pourable blend of food-grade mineral oil with a little beeswax. It soaks deep into dry wood, so it suits new, very dry or end-grain boards. The conditioner is a thicker wax balm that seals the surface and helps water bead. Most boards do best with oil first, then conditioner. See oil vs wax.

What kind of oil do you use?

Food-grade mineral oil (USP) in every product. It is clear, unscented and does not go rancid, unlike olive oil or other cooking oils.

How often should I condition a cutting board?

About once a month for a board in daily use, or whenever it looks lighter, feels dry, or water stops beading on it.
