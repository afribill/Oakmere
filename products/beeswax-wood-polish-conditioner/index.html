What it does

Oakmere Beeswax Wood Polish & Conditioner is a soft, easy-glide balm that does three jobs in one step. The food-grade mineral oil soaks into dry wood and deepens its colour. The Canadian beeswax fills fine scratches and dull patches and buffs to a warm, hand-rubbed glow. A little carnauba wax makes that glow firmer and longer-lasting.

It is silicone-free, so it will not leave a residue that gets in the way if a piece is ever refinished, and it is unscented. Use it on finished and raw wood alike.

How to use it.

All you need is a soft, clean cloth.

  1. Dust and clean

    Wipe the surface clean. Work on wood at room temperature, out of direct sun.

  2. Apply thin

    Rub a small amount into the wood with the grain, one section at a time.

  3. Wait

    Leave it 10 to 20 minutes so the wax and oil soak in.

  4. Buff

    Buff with a clean cloth until the surface feels smooth, not tacky.

Questions

Can I use the wood polish on finished furniture?

Yes. The Beeswax Wood Polish & Conditioner is made for finished and raw wood. On antiques, painted pieces or finishes you are unsure about, test a small hidden spot first.

The balm looks creamy rather than golden. Is that normal?

Yes. Natural beeswax blended with oil sets to a soft, creamy colour in the tin. It goes on clear and buffs to a warm glow.

My tin softened in a hot car. Is it ruined?

No. Beeswax softens in heat and firms up again once it cools. Store the tin closed, away from direct heat and sun.

Can I use Oakmere on wood floors?

We do not recommend waxing floors, because wax can make them slippery. Use a product made for floors instead.

How often should I polish furniture?

Every few months for most pieces, and more often for high-touch surfaces like dining tables and railings. Polish again when the wood looks pale or dry. Dust in between.
