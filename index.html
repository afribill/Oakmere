How to use Oakmere.

All you need is a soft cotton or microfibre cloth and about ten minutes.

  1. Dust and clean

    Wipe the surface clean. Work on wood at room temperature, out of direct sun.

  2. Apply thin

    Rub a small amount into the wood with the grain, one section at a time.

  3. Wait

    Leave it 10 to 20 minutes so the wax and oil soak in.

  4. Buff

    Buff with a clean cloth until the surface feels smooth, not tacky.

Full guide: how to polish wood furniture

  1. Wash and dry

    Hand wash the board, then let it dry completely, standing on its edge.

  2. Work it in

    Rub a layer over every face and edge, end grain included.

  3. Let it soak

    Leave it at least 20 minutes, or overnight for a new or very dry board.

  4. Wipe off

    Buff away anything that has not absorbed, and the board is ready.

What is the difference between the Cutting Board Oil and the Butcher Block Conditioner?

The oil is a pourable blend of food-grade mineral oil with a little beeswax. It soaks deep into dry wood, so it suits new, very dry or end-grain boards. The conditioner is a thicker wax balm that seals the surface and helps water bead. Most boards do best with oil first, then conditioner. See oil vs wax.
